# Body SOP

Run this on every paragraph under a headline or title: reason bodies, deks, offer blurbs, guarantee, close, FAQ answers, quote captions we write (not the VOC quote itself). Memory is not the SOP. This file is.

Dave, 2026-08-31, listicle. Body is where I hid the wanting because the titles did not say anything. Body is also where Grokbot fragments and punchy dares showed up. Body proves. It does not rescue a mute title.

## What body is

Hopkins: reason-why is the process, the material, the named proof that lets an intelligent reader agree. Ogilvy: the body is a stack of facts, not mood, once the headline has done the stop. If the body has to create the desire, the headline failed and you are padding.

## Tests (all must pass)

1. **Proves the line above.** Cover the headline. The body should still only be proof, specifics, and the next action. If the body is doing the wanting, go rewrite the headline. Do not add sentences to save it.
2. **Specific.** Time, stove, oil, bowl, steel, dishwasher, price, 30 days. "Theater-quality" and "genuinely beautiful" and "well-curated" fail. Hopkins: a definite fact is believed. A generality is not.
3. **Complete sentences.** No Grokbot fragments. The verb stays. "Every stove." is not body. "It works on gas, electric, or induction." is body, and it is still proof, not a headline.
4. **One job per paragraph.** Not hook plus proof plus offer plus wink. Kennedy: one step in the sale. The close paragraph may hold the offer. The reason paragraph may not.
5. **Friendly $200.** Persuade. Positive. Not a dare. Not a shove. Not "Trust us." She is spending about $229 on an impulse. Talk like a person in a kitchen, not a drill sergeant and not a brand PDF.
6. **Less clutter.** Make the statement. Stop. Partner hates extra words. Cut the clause that explains the feeling. Cut "That's the part owners tell us they didn't expect."
7. **Continue, do not recap.** Never "you just watched the video." After a microwave ad, do not retell the bag tear or the chemical gag. Kits include Artificial Butter Flavor. No ingredient-scare.
8. **True.** Live PDP bind. Real VOC, real first names from Junip, or Verified Buyer with a placeholder. No invented reviews, stats, SKUs, or Dennis B on three cards. No Save $89 if live is Save $98. No pot in our voice.
9. **Read aloud.** If it sounds like a landing-page generator, it is. If it sounds like a spec sheet that got lonely, the headline is still empty.

## Fail examples (do not rewrite these back in)

- Most kitchen gadgets get used twice and disappear into a cabinet. Here's why this one doesn't — in our words, with our customers' receipts.
- it's designed to complement a well-curated kitchen, not hide in a cabinet.
- theater-quality popcorn, every time.
- That's the part owners tell us they didn't expect.
- We dare you to go back to the bag.
- Join 100,000+ customers who made it a ritual.
- The spinner is the whole trick. (mechanism as the body lead under a mute title)
- The Popper makes it on your stove in about five minutes. It is stainless steel, and you will want to leave it out. (spec list with periods)

## Pass shape

Headline says she will be glad to leave it out.
Body: Multi-clad stainless, layered metal bottom, it stays on the stove. That is proof of the line above.

Headline says Friday got easy.
Body: The smell, the handle, about five minutes. Quote if we have a real one.

Offer body: The Popper in your color, 10 kits, $327 to $229, 30 days. That is the ask. It is allowed to be the ask because it is the offer block.
