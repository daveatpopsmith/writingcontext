# Kitchen island gate (required)

Dave, 2026-09-01: selling setup is not the miss. The miss is mechanical sentences, no prose, no warmth, and not talking to a 45-year-old woman.

Run this AFTER headline/title/body SOP and anti-AI. A line can pass every SOP and still fail this. If it fails, it does not ship.

## Who you are talking to

One woman. About 45. She already owns the good version of things. She does not need a worksheet, a dare, or a brand PDF. Talk the way you would talk across her kitchen island. Not to "women 40-70." Not to a persona name.

She is in `priority-personas.md` (Discerning Collector). Buyer files beat Sockeye. Sockeye is how we sound after she is in the room.

## Tests (all must pass)

1. **Her, not a segment.** Cover the product name. Would a 45-year-old woman with taste still feel spoken to, or would she feel targeted?
2. **Warmth.** Genuine. Inviting. Not fake warmth ("we're passionate"). Not prosecutor copy. Not a spec sheet with periods.
3. **Prose.** Sentences connect. Because, so, then. Not a stack of verdicts. Not three short punches in a row.
4. **Rhythm.** Mix long and short. If every sentence is the same length, it is a bot. Henneke: people do not talk in only short sentences.
5. **Read aloud.** If it sounds like a landing-page generator, it is. If it sounds like you are reciting a SOP, it is.
6. **Voice-over first.** Start from how we already talk (brand-voice, VOC, Dave's VO notes). Do not start from the SOP templates and then "add warmth."
7. **Hierarchy.** Having the fact is not enough. Decide which fact earns the space. Would a woman who never owned the competitor still feel the problem? A part number (two clips, nylon gears) is not a night in the kitchen. Dave 2026-09-01. See `/home/box/shared/copy-context/decisions/`.

## Fail shapes

- Complete, billboard, swap-test headlines that still sound like a product sheet
- Parallel scolds and stacked verdicts
- Crisp used as punchy
- Explaining her taste to her
- Justifying $229 to someone who does not justify kitchen objects
- Part-number H2s ("The lid is two clips") that a non-owner cannot feel

## Pass shape

She could hear it from a friend who already owns one. Specific. Warm. Finished thoughts. One idea flowing into the next.
