## What the talk excerpt does (Nigella / entertaining)

Host hands, not product hands. Abundance over variety so the guest is not afraid to take too much. Grapes on the mantel, clementines on shelves, Quality Street as decoration you eat. Plates piled, cutlery in a canister, barefoot so she is a better host. As a guest: pour wine, take round the sausages — “refill?” / “sausage?” instead of small talk. The night is how people move in the room. No object is trying to earn a cart.

## What the brand page does (Fellow / Eddy + Monty)

Sells by one avoided step made practiceable again. High-school milk burn → steaming is the scary skill → Eddy and Monty against cupboard mugs → first pours fail (foam left in the pitcher, spout too far) → steaming stays silky; art is still clumsy; she can see the night improving. How-to tips stay under the night. The objects earn their place because the burn-step feels safe enough to try again — not because a numbered latte-art lesson says so.
