# A specific claim is believed. A generality is not.

**Source:** Public summaries of Claude Hopkins, *Scientific Advertising* (1923), especially the "being specific" chapter recap at https://martinpollins.com/2024/03/26/scientific-advertising-claude-hopkins-masterpiece/ (fetched 2026-08-31). Platitudes roll off; a definite figure is taken at weight.
**Date added:** 2026-08-31

**Principle:**
If a claim is worth making, make it the way a careful person would check it. "Pure" meant nothing until Hopkins described bottles and filters. "Movie-theater popcorn" means nothing until oil, salt, time, and the bowl are in the sentence.

**Popsmith failure mode:**
"Theater-quality" with no kit, no time, no "no old maids." Borrowing "replaces 8 pans" logic from Our Place when this is one job, done well.

**One line we might run:**
No old maids. Not ever.

**Where this fails on Popsmith:**
That line is a customer line about unpopped kernels (@ColleenUnema-h8b in product knowledge). Use it as proof, not as a science lecture. Do not lead with the spinner to "explain" it.
