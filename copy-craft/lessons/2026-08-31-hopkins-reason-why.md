# Tell them why it is good. Not that it is good.

**Source:** Public Schlitz/Hopkins retellings: Fool on a Hill "Washed With Live Steam" (https://www.foolonahill.com/schiltz.html, fetched 2026-08-31); Rob Palmer on reason-why copy (https://robpalmer.com/blog/claude-hopkins-scientific-advertising-lessons, fetched 2026-08-31). Every brewer washed bottles. Hopkins was first to say so.
**Date added:** 2026-08-31

**Principle:**
Reason-why is the process, the material, the named proof that lets an intelligent reader agree. You do not need a unique factory. You need to be the first to say the true thing plainly.

**Popsmith failure mode:**
"Because we care" as the reason. Or the opposite: a cutaway of the spinner before she wants popcorn. Reason-why sits under a territory (Craftsman's Choice, Timeless Object), after the want.

**One line we might run:**
Heavy, solid, beautifully made. This is the Le Creuset of popcorn makers.

**Where this fails on Popsmith:**
Whirley-Pop comparison as a spec sheet. Brand guide: frame philosophy ("what kind of kitchen"), not a feature table, unless Roma's concept is literally that comparison.
