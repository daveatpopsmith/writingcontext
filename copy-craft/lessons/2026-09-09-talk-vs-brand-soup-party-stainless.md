## What the talk excerpt does (Heidi / soup party)

Scene first, then the object rides inside it. Neighbors host. Big pots. Guests bring a drink and one share. She sees a yellow-orange soup, asks Holly, goes home with a full belly and a cookbook under her arm. Hands are social (ask, send-home, tuck under arm) — not a feature tour. The soup is why she is still talking; the book is the souvenir of the night, not the pitch.

## What the brand page does (Made In / stainless)

Sells to her by staging one kitchen night around the object. Unused pan → someone asks → mushrooms → brown bits look like ruin → liquid returns → same night again. The scare and the try are the sell. Specs (wine/stock, cleaner, heat control) wait under the night. The pan earns the burner because the food changes.
