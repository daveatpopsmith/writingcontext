# One job per line. You, not we. CTA is a next action.

**Source:** Direct-response craft as taught in public conversion-copy primers (Unbounce conversion copywriting, https://unbounce.com/copywriting/conversion-copywriting/, fetched 2026-08-31; Copyhackers headline/VOC practice, https://copyhackers.com/blog/, fetched 2026-08-31). Hopkins's salesperson-in-print: grab, give a reason, ask. Ogilvy: every word counts.
**Date added:** 2026-08-31

**Principle:**
Each line earns one thing: stop, believe, or move. "You" is the subject. "We" is a support actor. The CTA names the next physical act (Shop the Popper / Shop gifts / Get the Starter Set), not a feeling ("Discover the magic").

**Popsmith failure mode:**
One sentence doing hook + proof + offer + wink. "We're a small brand on a mission" as the open. CTA "Learn more" or "Ready to upgrade movie night?" when the button already says Shop.

**One line we might run:**
Shop gifts.

**Where this fails on Popsmith:**
Stripping all "we" until the brand has no spine. Brand-guide Do allows "We dare you to go back to microwave popcorn after this." That "we" is a dare, not a bio. Keep it.
