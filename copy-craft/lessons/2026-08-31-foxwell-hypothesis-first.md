# Come in with the hypothesis. Do not ask the model for the pillar.

**Source:** Foxwell / Scalability School (Hannah Houg, Matthew Gattozzi), "Battling creative apathy in the age of AI" (https://www.foxwelldigital.com/blog/battling-creative-apathy-in-the-age-of-ai). Tarly note 2026-08-11. Practitioner interview. Not Popsmith data. Filed 2026-08-31.
**Date added:** 2026-08-31

**Principle:**
AI is fine for a first draft once a human has named the idea. It is poor at taste. Months of asking it to invent the hook atrophy the muscle. Volume without a hypothesis is twenty flavors of the same vanilla. Efficiency is not effectiveness.

**Popsmith failure mode:**
Peggy picking a territory because the page felt soft. Asking a model or AdNova to find the emotional job.

**One line we might run:**
If Roma did not name the territory and the hypothesis, stop. Do not invent the pillar.

**Where this fails on Popsmith:**
Dave can still ask for alternatives. Alternatives are riffs on a named job, not three new jobs.
