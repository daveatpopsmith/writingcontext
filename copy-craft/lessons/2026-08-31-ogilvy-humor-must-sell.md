# Humor that buries the sell is waste.

**Source:** Public quotations from *Confessions of an Advertising Man*, collected at https://www.antoinebuteau.com/lessons-from-david-ogilvy/ and https://swipefile.com/david-ogilvy-entertaining-vs-selling-quote (fetched 2026-08-31). Ogilvy's line: people do not buy from clowns; the job is to sell, not to entertain.
**Date added:** 2026-08-31

**Principle:**
A wink is allowed if the product is still the point. A joke that could be swapped onto any brand has failed. If she remembers the gag and not the Popper, you wrote a sketch.

**Popsmith failure mode:**
Kernel-mascot slapstick as the first three seconds. Explaining the wink ("see what we did there"). Bit-that-needs-a-setup on a 15-second ad. Brand guide wants Kristen's wink, not a clown.

**One line we might run:**
We dare you to go back to microwave popcorn after this.

**Where this fails on Popsmith:**
Killing all play to sound "premium." Padma's confidence still has a pulse. The test: would this line still sell if the joke were quieter?
