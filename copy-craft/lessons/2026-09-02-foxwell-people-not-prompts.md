# People, not prompts. AI UGC is not a pillar.

**Source:** Foxwell, "Why $1M Ads Still Start With People, Not Prompts" + go-deeper AI DTC WTF / Harry Delmege (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hPkwbKSg_hs). Tarly notes 2026-08-31 and 2026-09-08. Operator opinion, not Popsmith data. Filed 2026-09-02; deepened 2026-09-08.

**Date added:** 2026-09-02 · **Deepened:** 2026-09-08

**Principle:**
Ideation and research stay human. AI is execution (format skills, rewrite, grunt, impossible B-roll). The spark, angle, and hook are named by a person. A handful of ads that last months beat a week of concepts. AI UGC often spikes then dies in days; real people can hold. Volume is not the game — fewer bangers with a clear job. Human QA turns a 6/10 skill draft into a 9/10.

**Popsmith failure mode:**
Asking a model for the pillar. Briefing AI faces as a testing lane. Restacking volume instead of iterating 1-star / Marissa / Whirley. Pasting a full native long-form into a yapper skill. Shipping first AI draft without a human pass.

**One line we might run:**
No AI-generated people. Territory, hypothesis, and hook are named before anyone writes a variant. Prefer real UGC legs. AI formats after the job is named.

**How Roma uses this (operating):**
1. Concept / story / hook: human first. Peggy may use format skills only after the job is locked.
2. Testing lane: real talent (Courtney, Marissa, Whirley Tal) over AI UGC as a pillar.
3. Story test order (Delmege): native long-form first when it fits, then yapper — don’t paste the full native into the yapper.
4. Manual winning-ad notes stay on; verify AI claims.
5. Sibling lesson: AI around the work (`2026-09-07-foxwell-ai-around-the-work.md`) — Gattozzi on admin compression + living context docs.

**Where this fails:**
Impossible formats (claymation, Pixar) are his AI win. We do not run those as a Popsmith lane. Pet-niche AI animation is his path, not ours. Dave can still ask for riffs on a named job. Fatigue timelines and spend levels are his operator claims.
