# The ad is the product's personality.

**Source:** Bernbach public quotes via PAN Agency (fetched 2026-08-31): be provocative, but let provocativeness stem from the product; forget hard-sell vs soft-sell; say something with substance as it has not been said. Also his 1947 Grey memo, widely reprinted (Letters of Note, https://news.lettersofnote.com/p/let-us-blaze-new-trails, fetched 2026-08-31): persuasion is an art; techniques are not the idea.
**Date added:** 2026-08-31

**Principle:**
Voice is not a costume you put on a spec sheet. The Popper already has a personality: quiet confidence, a wink, a thing you leave out. The ad should sound like that object, not like a performance-marketer describing that object.

**Popsmith failure mode:**
Copy School template over the brand-guide roster. "We" paragraphs. Mascot voice (The Kernel / Palmer Lake) leaking into Meta primary text. Distinctive is not loud.

**One line we might run:**
Some things are too beautiful to put away.

**Where this fails on Popsmith:**
Inventing a new personality because this week's concept feels thin. Roma handed a territory. Stay inside it. Do not pull a Caraway "kitchen as character" film if the concept is a counter shot.
