# The brief is not the ad.

**Source:** Bob Hoffman, "The Three-Word Brief," The Ad Contrarian. Public text at https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/three-word-brief-bob-hoffman (©2023 Hoffman; recirculated as newsletter 2026-03-22). Standing homes: https://adcontrarian.substack.com/ and http://adcontrarian.blogspot.com/. Fetched 2026-09-01. Substack was a subscribe wall this morning. **Date added:** 2026-09-01

**Principle:**
People do not buy the strategy document. They buy the brand they already feel comfortable with. Hoffman says most "brand meaning," purpose, tribe, and co-create talk is a marketer fantasy. Positioning still has to be about something, but it is not the job. The job is a claim famous enough that she can choose you without a lecture. A great idea beats a perfect brief. Shipping the brief is how agencies hide.

**Popsmith failure mode:**
Writing Countertop Trophy, what we stand for, or a Le Creuset-for-popcorn essay onto the lander. That is the brief. She did not ask for it. Hoffman also wants mass fame. We do not. Godin already locked smallest viable: the woman who leaves beautiful things out, not snackers. Steal "don't ship the brief." Do not steal "make us famous."

**One line we might run:**
People come into the kitchen when it starts.

**Where this fails on Popsmith:**
Do not turn that line into a fame boast ("everyone knows us") or a gathering lecture. The line is what happens. Body still has to prove with time, steel, and the bowl. Roma names the territory. This is not a license to invent a pillar.
