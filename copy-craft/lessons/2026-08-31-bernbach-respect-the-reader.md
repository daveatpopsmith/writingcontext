# Respect the reader. Do not talk down.

**Source:** Public Bernbach recaps: Hanlon, "Reconnecting to Marketing's Human Side" (https://hanloncreative.com/hanlon-insight/hanlon-insight/reconnecting-to-marketings-human-side-with-lessons-from-legendary-creative-director-bill-bernbach/, fetched 2026-08-31); PAN Agency, "Bill Bernbach, the prophet of inbound marketing" (https://pan.agency/bill-bernbach-the-prophet-of-inbound-marketing/, fetched 2026-08-31). Treat her as an adult. Leave a gap she can fill.
**Date added:** 2026-08-31

**Principle:**
If you would not say it on her doorstep without sounding like a huckster, do not run it. No shouting. No "as a woman 40-70." No lecture on why popcorn matters. Wit that assumes she already gets it.

**Popsmith failure mode:**
Permission-selling ("you deserve a treat, busy mom"). Core-buyer concepts already killed that frame: she is not being sold to; she is being recognized. Rhetorical "Tired of microwave bags?" is talking down.

**One line we might run:**
Your kitchen already has the rest. Now it has this.

**Where this fails on Popsmith:**
Going so quiet the ad has no product. Respect is not mystery. She still needs a Popper in the frame and a CTA that is a next action.
