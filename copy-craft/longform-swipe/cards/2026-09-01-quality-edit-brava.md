# Gadget cynic, then the dinner that happened

**Source:** The Quality Edit, "Brava: The Smart Oven We're Swooning Over", https://www.thequalityedit.com/articles/brava-smart-oven-we-are-swooning-over, Scott Silver, published 2020-08-19, fetched 2026-09-01. Partnership advertorial.
**Who is talking:** A kitchen purist. Sinatra, mise-en-place, Cab for the fiancé. He already cooks. He hates gadgets (sous vide bags, unused ice cream makers).
**The walk:** Skepticism as identity → counter setup and an hour of onboarding → lamb chops, breakfast sandwich, broccolini. Because light can target, so a full tray cooks in zones, then the lamb was medium without him hovering. Subheads move the night, not a spec sheet.
**One steal:** Let the cynic stay in the room. The yes is what happened on the plate, not a feature stack.
**One never-steal:** Unboxing as charm, "#bravalife," numbered "more reasons," Jetsons costume, "investment" close, customer-segment roundup at the end.
**Collector:** yes. She already cooks well and is bored by kitchen toys. TQE is a pub she would open.
**Territory fit:** Craftsman's Choice. The object earns a counter because the food changed, not because it is new.
