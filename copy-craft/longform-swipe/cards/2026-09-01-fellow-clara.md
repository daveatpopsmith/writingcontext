# User pain, then the manufacturing that wouldn't line up

**Source:** Fellow journal, "Behind The Design: Clara French Press", https://fellowproducts.com/blogs/learn/behind-the-design-clara-french-press, Team Fellow / Drew Cosgarea, published date not on page, fetched 2026-09-01.
**Who is talking:** Product team, via the senior PM. They already tested other immersion brewers. Users already own a silt-and-cold French press.
**The walk:** AeroPress-like idea, then they switch because the 169-year press has real pain (silt, lining up the strainer, heat loss). Size, handle, lid too tight then too loose. Vacuum walls plus spout take six months because the weld won't seal and look right. They add a stir stick because people will use a metal spoon.
**One steal:** Let the failed lid and the six-month vacuum be the story. The feature is what the loop cost.
**One never-steal:** "Fellow fairy dust," Amazon-review mining as a flex, Q&A as chrome instead of a person in a kitchen.
**Collector:** almost. She already knows the press is silty. She would read why they rebuilt it.
**Territory fit:** Craftsman's Choice.
