# Live with it, then tell what the years did

**Source:** The Quality Edit, "My 7th Avenue Couch Is 3 Years Old, But It Somehow Looks Brand New", https://www.thequalityedit.com/articles/7th-avenue-couch-review-3-years-later, Hallie Katz, published 2026-08-26, fetched 2026-09-01.
**Who is talking:** A Collector who already bought the couch. She talks about buying fewer, better things as a habit she actually keeps. Then she moved.
**The walk:** Owns it three years. Downsizes. Flips the chaise five times. Takes the whole thing apart for a rug, alone. The white is wrong for the new room, so she changes the covers instead of the sofa. Then she understands modularity — she didn't, at purchase.
**One steal:** The review after use is the review. What happened in the room is the proof, not the launch unboxing.
**One never-steal:** "Invest in fewer, better" as a sermon, Cloud Couch dupe framing, white-glove as the product, permanence / looks-brand-new as the claim.
**Collector:** yes. Object already in the house. She rearranges a real room. TQE is her register.
**Territory fit:** Timeless Object as replacement (covers, not a new sofa). Not Craftsman's Choice.
