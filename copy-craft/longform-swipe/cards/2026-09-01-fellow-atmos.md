# Stale Friday coffee, then a pump that had to live in the lid

**Source:** Fellow journal, "A Q&A with the Inventor of Atmos Vacuum Canister", https://fellowproducts.com/blogs/learn/a-q-a-with-the-inventor-of-atmos-vacuum-canister, Drew Cosgarea, published date not on page (Atmos launch era), fetched 2026-09-01.
**Who is talking:** The engineer who started drinking coffee in college. Bag tastes off by Friday. Someone at the expo would use it if the pump weren't separate.
**The walk:** Class prototype with a hand pump → professor says it will be hard → fish-tank pump parts → Olympic-trials boredom and a patent → almost gives up when the money is gone → Fellow, then the curved pump in a Taiwan mold. Because oxygen steals the good compounds, so vacuum is the actual mechanism, not a gimmick canister.
