# Roommate mug on the counter, then a year of the holder

**Source:** Material Kitchen journal, "The Ultimate Holder of All Things", https://materialkitchen.com/blogs/design/the-ultimate-holder-of-all-things, brand journal, published date not on page, fetched 2026-09-01.
**Who is talking:** The brand as a person who used to cook. Tiny kitchen. Utensils in a roommate's mug. Rest in a drawer they opened twice a year. Takeout.
**The walk:** That mug got them this far. They're older. They spend a year on a wood crock because coiled-wire and plastic ones are ugly, and a twelve-knife magnet is too much if you only need three. Hidden magnets, rubber feet, a slant so tools fan. Because drawers wreck edges, so the knives stand in the wood.
**One steal:** Start in the ugly workaround she already has. The object is the grown version of that mug.
**One never-steal:** "We believe in form and function," "worth it" as price, FSC sermon, numbered little features as the piece.
**Collector:** almost. She already has knives she doesn't want in a drawer. Not millennial unboxing.
**Territory fit:** Craftsman's Choice. Timeless Object as replacement for the mug and the junk drawer.
