# Burn trauma, then the pitcher that lets her try again

**Source:** Fellow, "From Novice To Artist: Using Eddy and Monty For My Latte Art Journey", https://fellowproducts.com/blogs/learn/from-novice-to-artist-using-eddy-and-monty-for-my-latte-art-journey, Liz Dyer, fetched 2026-09-04.
**Who is talking:** A Fellow staffer who already pulls espresso and pour-over but has avoided steaming milk since a high-school burn scar. Not a beginner kit shopper. Not a barista-as-hero demo.
**The walk:** Skepticism is the scar and the aversion. Try is Eddy + Monty against the cupboard mugs, with tips from coworkers and YouTube. First pours fail (foam left in the pitcher; spout too far from the lip). Happened: steaming with Eddy is consistently silky; Monty's shape lifts crema when a normal mug sinks foam; art is still muscle memory after a pound of coffee, but she can see the night improving.
**One steal:** The object earns its place by making the scary step safe enough to practice. The burn is the night. Specs wait underneath.
**One never-steal:** Numbered steaming how-to as the H2. "Gift from the design gods" coworker tears. Product dump close.
**Collector:** almost. She feels a tool that finally lets her attempt the skill she has been ducking. Coffee gear, not Le Creuset cookware, keeps it from yes.
**Territory fit:** Craftsman's Choice. Soft Solo Moment: the morning she finally tries the art she avoided.
