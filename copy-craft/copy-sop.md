1. Brief: Roma named territory and hypothesis. If not, stop. Do not invent the pillar. Do not pull AdNova. Lander: wait for Roma's short ad summary (which working ad, which style). Do not pick the ad. Do not build the page.

4. `/workspace/popsmith-copy-craft/title-sop.md` on every kicker, page title, section title, card title, close title, FAQ question.
5. `/workspace/popsmith-copy-craft/body-sop.md` on every paragraph, dek, blurb, FAQ answer.
6. `/workspace/popsmith-copy-craft/anti-ai-tells.md` on the whole piece.
7. **Voice.** `/workspace/popsmith-contexts/brand-voice.md`. Joyful, Clever, Genuine, Crisp.
8. **Kitchen island (can veto a SOP pass).** `/workspace/popsmith-copy-craft/kitchen-island.md`. Warmth, prose, rhythm, one 45-year-old woman. Dave 2026-09-01.
