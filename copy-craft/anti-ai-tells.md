# Anti-AI tells (run before send)

A ruthless never-list. If a line trips any item, cut it. Do not explain why in the copy.

## Voice sludge

- [ ] Em dash habit. One can live. A stack is a bot. Prefer a period or a comma.
- [ ] "Here's the thing"
- [ ] "It's not X, it's Y"
- [ ] Synonym triples ("bold, beautiful, and brilliant")
- [ ] "Delve" / "unlock" / "elevate your"
- [ ] "In a world of"
- [ ] Filler adverbs: truly, incredibly, seamlessly, effortlessly, absolutely, uniquely
- [ ] Fake warmth: "we're passionate about"
- [ ] Explaining the joke after the joke
- [ ] General-noun headlines: "Experience the difference" / "A better way to snack"
- [ ] Rhetorical-question openers that stall: "Tired of microwave popcorn?"
- [ ] Stacked parallel clauses ("For the ones who gather. For the ones who host. For the ones who deserve more.")
- [ ] Ending with a question the CTA already answers ("Ready to upgrade movie night?" then Shop Now)

## Syntax tells

- [ ] Perfectly balanced three-beat lists in every paragraph
- [ ] "Whether you're X or Y"
- [ ] "For [persona], this is the [category] that [benefit]" as a headline. Positioning worksheet. Not kitchen-island speech.
- [ ] "More than just a [product]"
- [ ] "Imagine..." as a cold open
- [ ] "Let's dive in" / "At the end of the day" / "In today's fast-paced world"
- [ ] Hedging: "might just" / "could be the" / "perhaps the most"
- [ ] "Artisanal" / "elevate" / "reimagine" / "curated experience"
- [ ] Brand talking about itself in first-person plural for three sentences before the reader appears

## Popsmith-specific

- [ ] Do not lead with the spinner. Mechanism is proof, not the hook.
- [ ] Do not say "women 40-70" (or "women 45+", "Nostalgic Nester") in customer-facing copy. That is targeting language, not voice.
- [ ] Kernel is not Poppy. The Kernel is the mascot. Poppy Red left the range. Do not mix the names, and do not nickname the Popper "Poppy."
- [ ] No "you just watched the video." The lander is not a recap of the ad.
- [ ] No "the world's most advanced popcorn technology."
- [ ] No "impress your guests with artisanal popcorn."
- [ ] No "treat yourself to luxury" / "elevate your snacking experience."
- [ ] Do not invent reviews, ROAS, or AdNova numbers. Peggy does not pull AdNova.
- [ ] Do not invent the territory, the pillar, or the hook. Roma names those. Volume variants with no named hypothesis are vanilla.
- [ ] Do not call The Popper a pot. People hear sauce pot or stockpot. Say The Popper, or stainless steel stovetop popcorn maker.
- [ ] Telegraphic fragments that leave the verb out ("Every stove." "Stainless. Out."). A lander sentence has to finish the thought.
- [ ] Punchy dare-voice on a $200 lander ("We dare you to go back to the bag."). Friendly and persuasive. Not a shove.


## Headlines (reason-why numbers count)

Run `headline-sop.md`. If a line fails a test, it does not ship. Do not argue.

- [ ] Not a complete statement (noun phrase, caption, "It works..." bullet)
- [ ] Fails the billboard test (needs the body or the photo to mean anything)
- [ ] Does not SAY a thought. Specs and labels are not thoughts.
- [ ] Swap test fail: a kettle or a pan could wear the line
- [ ] Cover-the-body fail: the H2 cannot earn $229 alone
- [ ] Numbered reason is a spec (induction, dishwasher, stainless) or a clever recap of the enemy. The reason is the value. The spec is the proof.
- [ ] Recapping the ad's enemy as the headline after Zone 3 already fired


## Titles (kickers, section titles, card titles)

Run `title-sop.md`.

- [ ] Fake masthead (`Kitchen Essentials · Popsmith Review`)
- [ ] Generic lecture title (`Five Reasons the Popsmith Earns Its Place on Your Counter`)
- [ ] Module folder names (`Owners say it best`, `Value Propositions`)
- [ ] Title that repeats the H1 in softer words
- [ ] Spec chip used as a title (`Stainless steel`, `Dishwasher safe`)

## Body

Run `body-sop.md`.

- [ ] Body doing the wanting because the title did not (padding a mute headline)
- [ ] Generalities: well-curated, genuinely beautiful, theater-quality every time
- [ ] Explaining the feeling (`That's the part owners tell us they didn't expect`)

- [ ] Stacked verdicts with no person in them ("That was never cookware." "Owners do not invent this."). A prosecutor, not a kitchen.
- [ ] Parallel scolds ("You do not own other things like this. You do not braise in foil."). Quality Edit talk is a story with because/so/then, not a two-line slap.
- [ ] Recapping the ad, the bag tear, or ingredient-scare (kits include Artificial Butter Flavor)
- [ ] Offer + proof + wink in one reason paragraph
- [ ] Invented names, Save $89 vs live Save $98, three quotes with one name

- Gilker (or display) line + logo. Short title that names the night or the promise: Fridays She'll Remember · Quiet House. Perfect Popcorn · When They Already Have Everything · Heirloom Quality Popcorn Making.
