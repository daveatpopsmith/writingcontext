## What the voice actually does

- Names objects. Le Creuset, KitchenAid, the counter, the handle. Not "premium lifestyle."
- Lets the customer be a little skeptical. Price is allowed in the room.
- Sells the ritual and the object. Not the patented spinner as the opener.
- Wink, then stop. Do not explain the wink.

## What the bot does

- Abstracts ("experience," "elevate," "difference").
- Speaks for a demographic ("women 40-70") instead of to a person.
- Recaps the video on the lander.
- Confuses The Kernel (mascot) with Poppy (retired color).
