I had a Whirley Pop. It made okay popcorn, but getting it out was a mess. I had to shake it loose every time, so I used it once or twice and forgot it in the back of the cabinet. Another ugly, cumbersome kitchen gadget.

I almost didn't try The Popsmith Popper, because I already owned something that popped corn. Then I made a batch on the same burner as the rest of my cookware, and I understood what I'd been putting up with.

### You can't put it in the dishwasher.

Photo: Dave 2026-09-01. File `whirley-lander-assets/popcorn-vibes-in-the-new-kitchen.png`. Four-panel: Whirley Pop on the stove (disgust) vs Butter Popsmith Popper with gold handle (approval). Overlay: POPCORN VIBES IN THE NEW KITCHEN. This slot only. Face is in the meme on purpose.

You can't put a Whirley Pop in the dishwasher. It's aluminum, so the cycle will discolor it, strip the metal, leave a film, and corrode it. I wiped it with a paper towel, or I stood at the sink with this greasy, lightweight pan. I did both.

The Popsmith Popper goes in with the rest of the dishes. Lid, body, stirring arm. I don't wash it by hand anymore.

### The Whirley Pop is cheaply designed.

The gears are nylon and the lid is two clips, which is the whole mechanism. When the bowl fills, the crank gets heavy and the teeth skip. I've had those clips let go and dump popcorn on the burner. You can buy another lid when that happens.

### I hid it because I did not want anyone to see it.

It didn't belong in a modern kitchen, and it was awful to look at.

### Popsmith is the best popcorn I've ever made at home.

I put kernels and oil in The Popsmith Popper and turned the handle over medium heat. About five minutes later I had a full bowl, still hot, and people were already coming into the kitchen. Someone always wants the handle. This is the popcorn I want to serve.

### It is beautiful in my kitchen.

I use The Popsmith Popper all the time. People comment on it. I got the gold handles to match the fixtures in my kitchen, and cream because it sits against the backsplash. It lives on the stove.
