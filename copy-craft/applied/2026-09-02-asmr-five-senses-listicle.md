### Continues the ad

Like a conductor about to start a symphony, I know what's coming, even if everyone around me doesn't when I turn on the heat and put the Popsmith Popper down on the stove. Like an orchestra tuning its instruments I add the easy to pour corn, coconut oil, and butter salt from the all-in-one portion packs (The box came with 10!) and I'm ready to begin.

### 1. First, it's the sizzle.

The coconut oil heats up and hisses, starting to bounce a few of the kernels prepping them to explode. That's when I hear the first "Are you making popcorn?" from the TV room. The question has more hope and excitement than curiosity, nothing says 'you're a genius' like deciding to make homemade popcorn. Everyone loves it. I turn the handle a few times and then the kernels start to pop. One or two at first, dancing off the sides of the pot, and then a few more start to join in, moving around in a circle together on the Popper's spinner.

### 2. The house smells like a theater

When you walk into a movie theater right as they finish the first batch of popcorn? That smell is why I always loved matinee's as a kid, and now I get it at home all the time. The entire house now smells the buttery coconut oil and the nutty unmistakable scent of popping corn. Open a bag of microwave popcorn and its just steam and stale smells of bad cheap oil, you can't fool anybody after you've made popcorn in a Popsmith Popper.

### 3. I can't believe how much it makes.

I had to get a second bowl! So much fluffy popcorn. I popped the top off, a huge billow of steamy goodness revealed a massive treasure of fluffy yellow buttery kernels. More than expected and my bowl was definitely not big enough. It's so hard to resist grabbing that first handful of popcorn...so I usually don't (ha!) It's the reward to the chef, the popcorn expert and Popper Protector. I take this job very seriously. Eventually everyone's grabbing handfuls before I can even get to the bowl to the couch.

### 4. It's so fluffy!

You take a handful from the bowl and it feels light. Its not greasy. When I reach my hand into a microwave bag I always get yellow gunk on my knuckles, this feels crispy and airy but when you put it in your mouth? Flavor explosion. I have no idea how they do it. The longer it has to rest, the crunchier it gets, and I get get addicted for handful after handful.

### 5. Seriously, it tastes SO good.

Everyones addicted to this stuff. When friends come over? They want popcorn. Once I ran out of Popsmith Popcorn Packs and I had to use stuff I got at the store. They knew. This just TASTES different. It both tastes clean and fresh but also takes me back to eating popcorn in a sleeping bag when my Mom would cook it in cast iron pot when friends stayed over. Once I even ate the entire bowl by myself. It's that good. Crack a diet coke and call it dinner, what more do you need? This is what I want to serve and its what I want to eat. I'm never going back to popcorn any other way.
