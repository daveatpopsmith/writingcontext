# What people do not like about the Whirley Pop

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Job:** Source the us-vs-them rows. Do not invent reviews. Paraphrase. Do not paste books.

## What we can say because they printed it

Wabash Valley Farms owner manual / Home Depot PDF of the same booklet:
- Never place in a dishwasher.
- Never place an empty popper on a heated stove. The aluminum can melt.
- Forcing the handle when the popper is full will damage the gears and can break them.
- If you use it every week, washing is not necessary. Wipe with a paper towel.
- Lid held by two clips. Burn marks on the pan may not come off.

Their own troubleshooting page (whirleypopshop.com/trouble/): gears off the shaft, gears will not turn, stir wire does not turn, hard to turn and a weird noise, clips will not hold the lid shut, lid spins on the pan, clip broken, lid half broken off the bracket, pan dented, pan not sitting flat, pan out of round, burn marks. Fix for clips: bend them. Replacement lid sold with nylon gears or metal gears.

## What owners and reviewers actually say

- howtomakepopcorn.com (fetched 2026-09-01): complaints cluster on flimsy construction. Pot and lid described as "paper thin" or "like aluminum foil." Gears on the stirring mechanism are plastic and unreliable. Warranty exists; replacement parts still have shipping and handling.
- Serious Eats, Kenji López-Alt, "Why I Love My Whirley Pop" (fetched 2026-09-01): a thin six-quart aluminum pan with a snap-on lid and a crank. Dishwasher is not recommended. He mentions a thin pan and water in the crank. Dave 2026-09-01: do not say gears trap water. Dishwasher damage is aluminum: discolor, strip the metal, film, corrosion. He calls it a one-trick pony you have to store. Do not paste the piece. Steal the facts: thin aluminum, no dishwasher, you hide it.
- Trustpilot on the shop (secondary, service mixed with product): clips slip free, the pan falls, popcorn hits a hot burner. Use as a clip/lid problem, not as a customer-service roast.

## Axes that are true on both sides

Whirley Pop: thin aluminum, nylon gears, two clips, hand wash only (dishwasher discolors, strips metal, films, corrodes aluminum), you put it away. Do not say gears trap water.
The Popper: multi-clad stainless, stirring arm, lid stays on, dishwasher, you leave it out. About five minutes to a full bowl.
