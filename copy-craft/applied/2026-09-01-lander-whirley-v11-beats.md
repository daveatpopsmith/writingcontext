I had a Whirley Pop that made okay popcorn, which would have been fine if getting it out weren't such a mess. I had to shake it loose every time, so I used it once or twice and forgot it in the back of the cabinet. Another ugly, cumbersome kitchen gadget.

I almost didn't try The Popsmith Popper, because I already owned something that popped corn. Then I made a batch on the same burner as the rest of my cookware.

### I stood at the sink with it.

Photo: Dave 2026-09-01. File `whirley-lander-assets/popcorn-vibes-in-the-new-kitchen.png`. Overlay: POPCORN VIBES IN THE NEW KITCHEN. This slot only.

I wiped it with a paper towel, or I stood at the sink with this greasy, lightweight pan. I did both. Everything else I own goes in the dishwasher. This couldn't. It's aluminum, and the cycle will discolor it, strip the metal, leave a film, and corrode it.

The Popsmith Popper goes in with the rest of the dishes. Lid, body, stirring arm. I don't wash it by hand anymore.

### I've dumped popcorn on the burner.

I've had the lid let go in the middle of a batch. Popcorn on a hot burner. You don't forget that smell, and you don't want it next to enamel. The gears are nylon and the lid is two clips, and when the bowl fills the crank gets heavy and the teeth skip. You can buy another lid. It's cheaply designed.

### I hid it because I didn't want anyone to see it.

The Dutch oven stays on the stove. This went in a cabinet. It didn't belong in a modern kitchen, and it was awful to look at.

### Popsmith is the best popcorn I've ever made at home.

I put kernels and oil in The Popsmith Popper and turned the handle over medium heat. About five minutes later I had a full bowl, still hot, and people were already coming into the kitchen. Someone always wants the handle. This is the popcorn I want to serve.

### It is beautiful in my kitchen.

I got the gold handles to match the fixtures, and cream because it sits against the backsplash. People comment on it. I use it all the time. I'd miss it if I put it away. It lives on the stove, next to the cookware I actually leave out.
