# Five felt nights (before talk files)

Buyer: Discerning Collector in priority-personas.md. She already owns Le Creuset. She leaves beautiful things out. She does not justify. She wants people to see what she keeps.
Brand: Joyful, Clever, Genuine, Crisp. It belongs next to Le Creuset because she keeps the good version visible.
Job: why a tired popper does not belong on her stove.
Reader: a woman who never owned a Whirley still has to see these nights.

1. She hides it. The Dutch oven stays on the stove. This goes in a cabinet because she does not want anyone to see it.
2. She stands at the sink with a greasy pan after everything else she owns went in the dishwasher.
3. Food hits a hot burner. She does not forget that smell. She does not want it next to enamel.
4. She used it twice and forgot it. She does not forget the things that belong in this kitchen.
5. The one she keeps: people come into the kitchen when it starts. She would miss it if she put it away. It lives next to the Dutch oven.

Not these, not in the headline: two clips, nylon gears, aluminum, part numbers.
