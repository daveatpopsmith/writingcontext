# Caroline Chambers, What To Cook — I wrote you another book

Source: https://whattocook.substack.com/p/i-wrote-you-another-book
Date: published 2026-02-23; captured 2026-09-01

How they talk: Kitchen-person, caps and extra exclamation points. She talks to "you guys." Warmth is the screenshots of dinners, not a chef line. Complete-meal, walk-away, 15 minutes. Pre-order sits at the end and is not the cadence.

Rhythm: Pride. Then I heard you. Then the three chapters. Then the life it fits. Then xoxo. Skip the tour ask for mimic.

One steal: Say you heard how dinner actually happens, then meet her in the 15 minutes she has.

Never steal: Pre-order urgency. Book-tour logistics. Recipe roundups. Do not contact her.

Who is in the room: Busy home cooks, often parents, who still want the food to be good. Public post (not a paywalled Saturday recipe).
