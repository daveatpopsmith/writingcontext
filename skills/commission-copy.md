## Course (before research, any subject)

Do this live, not in retrospect.

1. From the ad, the buyer, and what the page has to catch, write one sentence with people in it: who is in the other room, and what her hands are doing. That sentence is the night.
2. If the sentence is a category (five senses, a listicle, five features, a format), it fails. Rewrite until it has another person and her hands. Do not harvest yet.
3. From `/home/box/shared/copy-context/popsmith-purpose.md` + that night sentence, write one hard `Emotional-core:` line: why this night / this buy matters for her (want, experience, results, worth, trust, fun, her way). Fail if it is how-it-works, reviews, brand-voice tips, or mechanics.
4. Tarly's brief is the night sentence, not the format. Capture-once whole paragraphs of how people already talk about that night. A neighboring library is not a substitute.
5. Read the first few excerpts before Peggy opens anything. If there is no house, no other person, and no hands, the slice is wrong. Kill it and harvest again.
6. Only then: packet (agenda is the night; Emotional-core filled), spine (spoken lines about that night; at least one carries Emotional-core), compile. Gate is night vs explanation **and** core vs how-it-works. Glue is not the test.
7. When Dave rewrites, that rewrite is the ship and the floor. Diff keep vs leave into decisions/. Do not compile over it.

## When a writing job appears

1. Write the night sentence first. Fail it if it is a category.
2. Write Emotional-core from popsmith-purpose.md + the night. Fail if mechanics/how-it-works.
3. If the night-slice is missing, Tarly fills it from that sentence. Check the first excerpts. Then write the job packet in `/workspace/popsmith-copy-craft/jobs/YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md` from `/home/box/shared/copy-context/JOB-PACKET.md`.
4. Fill: piece, agenda (the night sentence), Emotional-core, standing buyer/brand, contrast as felt nights, must-keep, cannot-headline, named libraries including the night-slice, Roma summary if a lander, Roma brief if an ad.
5. Do not send Peggy until Emotional-core is present and non-mechanical, the night-slice passed the first-excerpt check, and standing talk libraries hold whole paragraphs.
6. Message Peggy with the packet path. She runs [Popsmith copywriting](sand-workflow:popsmith-copywriting). Talk stays closed until she files `Spine:`.
7. Spine fails if it is category labels, part numbers, announcing a show, or pure how-it-works with no Emotional-core. Send it back. Do not let her draft.
8. Draft gate before Dave sees it. Night vs explanation. Core must land (want/experience), not only process. Named fail: three unlinked handle/lid lines; draft that only teaches corn/salt/oil. Positive bar: Dave's rewrites in decisions/.
9. Put only the piece in Dave's chat.
