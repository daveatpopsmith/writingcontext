## Compiler

1. Read standing buyer + brand + purpose + the job packet. Do not open a library.
2. Confirm Emotional-core is present and is want/experience (results, worth, trust, fun, her way) — not corn/salt/oil process. If missing or mechanical, stop and send back to Hagen.
3. Write five story beats as spoken lines she would say. Each beat is a night a stranger can see. At least one beat must carry the Emotional-core. Sense names, part numbers, announcing "I am putting on a show," and pure how-it-works fail. File under `Spine:`.
4. If a beat explains the object instead of being the night, rewrite the spine. Do not draft.
5. Read every file in the library the packet named. Whole paragraphs. Not notes/, steal lists, or short attributed phrases. The library cannot change the agenda. Those paragraphs are the cohesion bar.
6. Draft. SOP files, then kitchen-island.md. Island can veto. Do not force first-person confession if the packet did not ask for it. Simile only if the next sentence uses her hands. H2s are spoken. People coming in is dialogue once, not restaged. Senses may bleed. Close lives in the last graf if the packet says so. How-it-works stays thin (tease); the piece must make her want the experience.
7. Cohesion gate before you file. Every sentence follows from the last. Describe what is happening. Do not stack punchy lines that assume the reader already knows. Named fail: "That would ruin it. I keep turning the handle. The lid stays on until it is done." Positive bar: Dave's 2026-09-02 five-senses rewrite (`/workspace/popsmith-copy-craft/applied/2026-09-02-dave-five-senses-rewrite.md` and `decisions/annotated/2026-09-02-dave-five-senses.md`).
8. Emotional-core gate: if the draft only teaches process / features / reviews and never lands why she wants the night, rewrite or stop. Do not file.
9. Never call The Popper a pot (unless Dave already wrote pot on that page). Never paste the library. Continue the ad's POV.

## Done when

The packet has Emotional-core and a spine of spoken nights that passed, she has read the named library, the draft is a scene a stranger can follow that lands the want/experience (not only how-it-works), island passed, territory named if it is an ad.
