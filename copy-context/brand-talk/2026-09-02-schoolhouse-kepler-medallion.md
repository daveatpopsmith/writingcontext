# Kepler Medallion

The Kepler Medallion's layered composition of brass and wood materials are an ode to the astronomer, Johannes Kepler. A bronze tinted glass shade adds a layer of warm translucency that reflects light across its high-gloss surface, while an elegant solid White Ash medallion highlights the beauty of its natural wood grain. Perfect as both a surface mount or wall sconce, this celestial-inspired light fixture can be statement-making or subtly sophisticated depending on the context. Due to its natural material, variations in wood grain will occur. A Schoolhouse Exclusive.

Wood component made from 100% FSC Certified White Ash. Woodgrain unique to each fixture due to grain variation. Processed and assembled in Portland, Oregon using ethically-sourced domestic and global components.
