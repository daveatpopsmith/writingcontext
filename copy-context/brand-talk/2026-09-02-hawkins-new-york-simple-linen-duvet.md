# Simple Linen Duvet

RUMPLED JUST RIGHT. 100% European linen; finished, woven, and stonewashed in Portugal. Oeko-Tex Standard 100 certified. 210 gsm weight. Branded buttons for hidden closure and 4 ties in the corner so your insert won't budge. Generously sized for a perfect drape. We love to mix and match.

Our Simple Collection is all about pared back design, made from tried and true materials that last and last. These pieces are versatile and useful, designed with an easiness that makes them seem at home in traditional or minimalist spaces, and can make small moments feel nice and big ones feel… well, simple.

We love linen for bedding because it’s breathable and soft, keeping you both cool in the summer and warm in the winter. It’s all generously sized, weighty linen - perfect for draping and creating an unfussy, elegant bed. Our linens are made from high-quality flax, sourced in Belgium. They’re then finished in Portugal, where they’re woven, stitched, dyed, and stonewashed for that signature Hawkins softness - which we love to remind you only gets softer over time.
