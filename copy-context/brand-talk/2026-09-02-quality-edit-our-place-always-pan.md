# Why This Pan Keeps Selling Out

In partnership with Our Place.

I've had a love of cooking for as long as I can remember. Fast forward to a few years ago to my folks downsizing our childhood home. As part of the move, I inherited a collection of Le Creuset cookware, which in my eyes became the gold standard for use in the kitchen. And so when my friend mentioned she was hearing buzz about Our Place, my first thought was skepticism.

How could this new upstart compete with Le Creuset's nearly 100 years of french culinary prestige? I decided to humor my friend and give Our Place's Always Pan a spin in my home kitchen and I can honestly say the product lives up the hype.

The Always Pan is a multi-functional patent-pending stovetop system designed to replace the clutter of a 16-piece cookware set with one innovative design that does it all. In all honesty, Our Place delivered on multiple levels.

The Always Pan replaced my 16 piece cookware set.

At $145, it's a fraction of the price of some of the luxury tier cookware on the market, especially when you factor in its versatility. I've used it for everything -- sautéing, frying, steaming, boiling and I swear it does it all flawlessly. As for cleanup -- scrubbing the crust off my other frying pans was the bane of my existence.

It's a lot easier to consistently cook when you're using great tools that make your life easier in the kitchen. The verdict: I'm sold and kinda sad such a beautiful (and functional) pan wasn't in my life sooner.
