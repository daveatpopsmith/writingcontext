# Our Place's New Always Pan Pro Is Going To Change How You Cook Forever

In partnership with Our Place.

Moving in with a significant other typically comes with a bit of a learning curve. My husband (a Food Network devotee and once-upon-a-time culinary school graduate) and I (happy to stick to the basics when it comes to cooking) faced a pretty steep one when it came to our kitchen.

We quickly learned that, while we were combining many facets of our lives, it was okay for us to keep things separate in the cookware department (perhaps you can imagine the conversation we had after I attempted to make pancakes on high heat in his beloved stainless steel pan). He keeps his set of stainless steel pots and pans in one drawer; my easy-to-use and easy-to-clean non-stick pan lives in another.

But, when I saw that Our Place was coming out with an Always Pan Pro — designed to combine the searing power of stainless steel with the ease of a nonstick — I wondered if I'd finally found a pan that would work for us both (and help us declutter our redundant kitchen). I snagged one (quickly — the brand's pans always sell out) to find out. Here's what happened…

So, is this pan really all that it's cracked up to be? In short, it's more impressive than either of us could have ever expected. First and foremost, we both agree that we love how it looks (and even let it sit on our stovetop when it's not in use!). For once, we're also on the same page about cooking with it, too.

From my perspective, I now understand why serious chefs are so obsessed with stainless steel pans. I am not exaggerating when I say that my food literally tastes better when I cook it in this pan. I can get those delicious crispy edges I had waved goodbye to when I chose the non-stick-pan life.

And my husband has actually incorporated it into his repertoire, too! He's not exactly one who embraces change without good reason. I think he enjoys being able to toss it into the dishwasher and not having to obsessively care for it to ensure that it continues to perform. Basically, we're all winners when we cook with the Always Pan Pro.
