# Popsmith purpose (standing — Dave Stickland, 2026-09-15)

**Status:** Locked source of truth for emotional core. Hagen applies; does not invent. Alfred filed.

## What this is
Why someone spends money with us — the true job of The Popper / why the night matters. Not ad mechanics. Not a how-it-works script.

## Order of operations (why they buy)

1. **The results** — The popcorn tastes legitimately great. You can’t get that at the grocery store (we won’t say that in ads directly).
2. **Worth the price** — Well made, lasts a long time, beautiful. A talking point in the kitchen. An investment. Something to show off. Fun.
3. **Easy + high trust** — Easy to use, easy to clean up, easy to trust the ingredients aren’t full of junk.
4. **Fun** — Novel. Universally loved snack. Everyone loves popcorn — how can you not?
5. **Your way** — Infinite ways to enjoy it. Goes with everything.

## How-it-works (not purpose)
Process of making it is simply a how-it-works. Tease in images. In an ad you may say something like: easy to use — just add corn, salt, and oil, and have popcorn in a few minutes. Any more than that belongs on a landing page.

## Ad goal (Dave)
Get them to want the popcorn machine and be willing to learn more because they want the **experience**. Ads make them interested in eating popcorn and in experiencing making it — because microwave popcorn, bagged popcorn, and popcorn from terrible cheap machines is a waste of time.

Every job packet needs one hard `Emotional-core:` sentence drawn from this page + this piece’s night. Not mechanics, not reviews, not brand-voice tips, not a how-it-works dump.
