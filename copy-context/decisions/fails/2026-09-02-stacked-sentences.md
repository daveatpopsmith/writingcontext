# Fail: stacked sentences that assume

Dave, 2026-09-02, five-senses listicle. Same miss he already locked on the Whirley lander.

Named line: "That would ruin it. I keep turning the handle. The lid stays on until it is done."

Why it dies:
- Three statements. No scene.
- No connectivity. The next sentence does not follow from the last.
- Assumes the reader already knows what "it" is, why calling would ruin it, and what the lid has to do with the handle.
- Not describing. The Tarly whole-paragraph saves describe. This stacks.

Replace with narrative: she hears the sizzle, she does not call anyone because the sound is the invite, she keeps turning so it does not burn, the lid stays on because the kernels are still going and you cannot eat it yet. A stranger can see the kitchen.

This is standing. Not a one-piece note. Hagen sends it back. Dave does not teach it again.
