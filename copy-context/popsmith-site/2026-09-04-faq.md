Fetched: 2026-09-04
Claims touched: 100% Dishwasher Safe; Backed By a 1 Year Warranty; Makes Savory AND Sweet Popcorn; Multi-clad stainless steel design (paint/stainless)

## Excerpts

> Popsmith is the secret to making perfect movie theater popcorn and homemade kettle corn at home. Making stovetop popcorn has never been this fun and tasty.

> Is the Popper dishwasher safe?
> It is! You can put all of its parts inside the dishwasher, including the spinner.

> What is the Popper's warranty?
> We offer a one-year warranty on the Popsmith Popper.

> Will the paint chip off over time?
> The Popsmith is made of all stainless steel with multiple layers of heat resistant paint adhered to its body. However, like any stovetop cookware, treat it with care. Aggressive use may cause the Popper to scuff.

> Can I use the Popper on an induction stove?
> Yes, the Popsmith is compatible with all stovetops and cooktops, including Induction stoves.

> How do I prevent my Kettle Corn from burning?
> Start by adding the coconut oil and popping corn from your pre-measured popcorn kit. When the oil has fully melted and you start to hear (and smell) that sizzle, add Sugar'n Salt mix. Close the lid and give your Popsmith a few more turns to mix the ingredients together. (Or give your Popper a little shake to ensure the bottom kernels don't over-caramelize or burn!)
