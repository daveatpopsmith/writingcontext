> Care & Use
> Warm your Popsmith Popper on medium heat. Add 2-3 tablespoons of coconut oil and 4oz of Popsmith kernels. Once popping begins, pour 2-3 tablespoons of Kettle Corn Mix or Choc-O-Pop Powder, shut the lid, and start spinning the crank. Remove from burner once popping slows to 1-2 seconds, then pour into a bowl and enjoy!
