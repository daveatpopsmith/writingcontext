> The Popsmith Popper was built to last a lifetime. It's crafted from premium stainless steel with a multi-clad metal bottom, giving it the following advantages:

> Durability: There's a clear difference when you hold the Popsmith. The 18/8 stainless steel body feels solid and substantial — it's the kind of kitchen tool you'll pass down to your kids one day.

> Even heat distribution: The multi-clad metal bottom ensures a consistent pop every time, preventing burnt and unpopped kernels.

> Dishwasher safe: After popping, you can just load the Popper into the dishwasher and enjoy your snack without dreading the cleanup after.
