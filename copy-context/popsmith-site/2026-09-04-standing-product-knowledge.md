> Key Features:

> Materials:
> All stainless steel construction

> The patented stirring mechanism is the core of the Popper's unique design and performance. It is engineered to solve the two biggest problems with stovetop popcorn: burning and unpopped kernels.

> Floating Design: The stirring mechanism floats just above the bottom of the pot. It does not scrape the surface.
> Gentle Agitation: As the handle on the lid is turned, the mechanism spins around the outside of the pot, gently moving the kernels around the bottom.
> Lifting Action: As the popcorn pops, the mechanism lifts the popped kernels off the hot bottom surface, preventing them from burning while allowing unpopped kernels to remain in the hot zone.

> All three components of the Popper — the pot, the lid, and the internal stirring mechanism — are dishwasher safe. The stirring mechanism is easily removed for cleaning.

> Kettle Corn Powder: A powder that allows customers to make sweet kettle corn at home, a feature not possible with many competing poppers like the Whirley-Pop.
