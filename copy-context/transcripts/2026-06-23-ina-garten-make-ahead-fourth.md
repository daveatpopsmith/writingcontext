The Fourth of July is a great time to have the kind of party that I love: a relaxed atmosphere and food that is familiar and delicious.

I always like to use recipes that let me prepare parts of the meal in advance, and then I’ll have guests help with the rest. Below is a great menu that really lets you focus on your company, which is what a party is all about!

Three Days before the party, I buy the ingredients and make the caramelized onions. On July 3rd, I prepare the salads, bake the cake, and mix and form the hamburger patties.

All that’s left to do on July 4th is set a buffet table, make the cake frosting, and assemble the cake! I ask my friends to help cook the burgers when they come over, and I make sure everyone always has a cold beer or lemonade (store-bought is fine!).

Your friends will feel involved in the celebration, and no one has to panic about entertaining!

Always check the seasonings on salads you make ahead, because salt and pepper lose their flavor over time.
