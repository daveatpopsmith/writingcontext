Before honing in on the art of living, Calderone explored many creative avenues from acting to yoga, according to The New York Times. She eventually took classes on interior design at Parsons and cooking lessons at culinary school. In 2011, she blended those passions by starting EyeSwoon as a Tumblr account. Calderone blogged about recipes and interiors as she and her husband, Victor, a music producer and DJ, fell into a rhythm of renovating and selling homes. This pursuit dates back to their first loft together in Brooklyn's Dumbo neighborhood, which they moved into circa 1998.

"We weren't flippers," Calderone told Architectural Digest in 2018. "The reason we became serial movers is because our needs kept changing. Each time we amplified our space, it also amplified my interest in design."

When the couple and their son moved into a town house in Brooklyn's Cobble Hill neighborhood, the property became the eighth home they renovated together. The Greek Revival residence ignited endless Pinterest pins following our inside look at it in 2018.

Calderone is known for crafting marble-clad, neutral interiors with European influence. In her now former Cobble Hill home and current Amagansett, New York, beach house, she created airy kitchens with boldly veined marble and Parisian-style open shelving. The Cobble Hill home's romantic primary bathroom—with a pink marble shower, freestanding tub, and Murano glass chandelier—was once one of Pinterest's most-pinned bathrooms. She's also known for favoring predominately white rooms with tall black-framed windows, wiry light fixtures, artful Italian sofas, and organically shaped vases filled with branches.

"People always say, 'How do you keep that marble spotless?'" she told AD in 2020. "And I tell them, 'I want to see those stains. I want to remember those moments when my son spilled his ice cream.' It's a marker; it's a memory. I love the idea of something with a rich, unknown history starting a new life in your space."
