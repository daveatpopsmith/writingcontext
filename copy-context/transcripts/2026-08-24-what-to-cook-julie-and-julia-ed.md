Kelsey Cody is a friend I met by way of Instagram. She cooked every. single. recipe. from What To Cook When You Don’t Feel Like Cooking book number one in the span of seven months. She’s a professional photographer and took pictures of every meal. She’s also the mom of two girls, who were both under 2 years old when she challenged herself to cook her way through the whole cookbook!

I got to meet her last week on the Make It Fast tour. She drove an hour to be there and I asked her to come backstage to give me a hug as soon as she arrived.

I am 1) so honored that she chose my book to cook through, and 2) so impressed!!! That is a LOT of home cooking. Kelsey has shared some fun and inspiring takeaways from her experience with me, and, today, we’re passing them along to you, too!

I was three months postpartum with my second baby. I had two under 2 and was just a little… frazzled? I wanted to do something that made me feel like me. I had purchased the ingredients for the lamb & hummus bowls, and while I was thinking about dinner, I just decided that I’d cook through the book. It was a quick decision. I told my husband, posted it on Instagram, and the rest is history.

And yes! Julie & Julia is my comfort movie. When I’m not feeling well or am having a rough day, it’s the movie I put on. I have watched it over and over since it came out and had always wanted to cook through a cookbook. I actually did it once before in 2021, and had wanted to do it again for a while.
