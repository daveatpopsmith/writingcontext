I love a dessert table with lots of goodies. Since it’s almost Easter, I’m sending the people I love a few of my favorite desserts from Goldbelly.

This way, all they have to do is put together a dessert buffet and decorate! There’s something really wonderful about a party where you help yourself.

---

I believe in having stress-free dinner parties, but that’s not always easy with a big group! One way to help simplify dinner is to serve platters, not plates. I love to arrange a buffet table.

After the stories are told, you can ask a few guests to help bring out bowls of Matzo Ball Soup. Then I like to put everything on a buffet table nearby—big platters of Brisket with Onions and Leeks, Garlic Roasted Potatoes, Whitefish Salad, Oven-Roasted Vegetables, wine, and sparkling water. Guests can help themselves and each other, and it really makes the holiday feel like a shared experience.

You’ll already have a beautiful centerpiece with the Seder plate and candles, but you can add bowls of pomegranates, oranges, or figs. I like the centerpiece to feel like part of the meal.

Passover doesn’t have to be too sophisticated!
