I'll cut to the chase: You've got to make this soup. I could wax poetic about its deep, multi-layered flavor, and rave about how satisfying it is — and add that if you have one of those big bag of greens in the fridge that absolutely must be cooked TODAY, then this is how to use it. But even that would undersell it, so please, just take my word on this and make Caroline Chambers's Hella Green Soup.

The recipe came about after Caro's Hella Green Pasta recipe blew up, back in 2023. "I've probably gotten a thousand messages, saying something along the lines of, 'This sauce is so good we're eating it like soup!'" she explains. "So…I made a hella green soup!" This dish, from her latest cookbook (out next week!), does the same magic trick as the sauce, condensing a whopping pile of vegetables into a harmonious spoonful of flavor. "Heavy cream brings richness into what could otherwise be labeled a blended salad," she adds.

The first time I cooked it, my six-year-old came into the kitchen and said, "Whatever you're making, I want to eat it right now." She had three bowls. My point being: Make this, and make it a double.
