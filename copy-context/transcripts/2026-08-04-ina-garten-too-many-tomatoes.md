There’s really nothing like the flavor of fresh summer tomatoes, whether they come from the market or your garden. I love waiting for my plants to ripen, but then I’m suddenly overwhelmed with fruit! It’s why you can find so many salad recipes with tomatoes in my cookbooks.

As soon as August comes, I feel like making a gorgeous Panzanella, which is a classic Italian tomato, cucumber, and bread salad. The Panzanella recipe in my book Parties! is great because it uses vegetables and herbs that are in season at the same time. I also have a Grilled Panzanella that turns up the volume by using heirloom tomatoes and thick slices of grilled bread instead of croutons. It’s really good.

Making a chilled soup like Gazpacho is a great way to enjoy tomatoes at their freshest. When I have too many, I make my Roasted Tomato Basil Soup and freeze it in quart containers so I can have it when there’s a chill in the air. It’s easy to reheat and hot-rod with Grilled Cheese Croutons.

“Hot-rod” usually refers to an old car that’s been souped-up with a new engine or parts to make it go faster. In my upcoming cookbook, Simply Ina, I talk about how you can hot-rod leftovers or store-bought prepared foods the same way!

I also just love to keep a bowl of cherry tomatoes on the counter for anyone to snack on as they ripen. And overflowing bowls of ripe tomatoes are a beautiful centerpiece at a summer dinner party!
