What do you remember about the first time you ever played host?

If I recall correctly, I was 5 years old and I invited all of my sister’s barbie dolls over to my side of the room for an afternoon tea. They didn’t eat much, but the conversation was lovely!

If someone is brand new to party throwing, what 3 things would you suggest they acquire before hosting?

1. A tablecloth or a few yards of fabric to dress up their dining table.
2. Food storage containers for prepping parts of recipes ahead of time—like sauces, vegetables, and more.
3. Pantry basics. Make sure your essentials are well stocked and ready for cooking. They will get you through any issues you have in prepping the menu!

What size group makes for your ideal gathering?

6-8 people, I think, for a seated dinner party! More than that and it gets a little unruly and hard to have multiple conversations going around the table. Plus, it’s the right amount of people to cook a nice meal without having to do too many tricks in the kitchen. I like a nice, manageable menu for 6-8 people, like the ones in Let’s Party! If I’m hosting a larger group, I’ll spread out the food and the seating arrangements to encourage mingling.

Kids at the party, yeah or nay?

Oh yes, I love kids and I love watching kids' faces while they eat my food! I get photos of kids covered in my vodka sauce or tearing into my meatballs all the time. My parents never made me special “kids” food when I was little, and I’m heartened to see parents doing the same thing today. That’s how they’re treated at my house, too!

Best time for a party to start? And when to stop?

I’m an early girl (like the tomato), so I usually have people come over around 6:30, start feeding them by 7, and then gently start telling them to leave around 9:30.

What’s the right way to dress for a dinner party at Dan’s house?

There is no right way, and thus there is no wrong way!!! Come as you are, as long as you’re hungry!

Be honest, what’s the weirdest / least cool thing you’ve ever experienced from a host at a party you were attending?

I once arrived at a dinner party at the invited time of 7:30pm to find the host just preheating the oven and unwrapping a chicken to prepare for roasting. I immediately knew we would not be eating until 10pm and almost had an anxiety attack.

Would you be willing to give us a 5-song dinner party playlist?

Oh man, I always ask someone else to put on a playlist! I’m all about leaning into your strengths when hosting and asking for help with your weaknesses. I’m not to be trusted with music because I only listen (and scream out loud) to broadway musicals.

How should a good host indicate that the party is over?

I address this very important issue in Let’s Party! I stand up and say, “This has been so fun, but it’s approaching my bed time.” Protect your peace above all things! I think the key is realizing that the people who want to keep partying will find somewhere else to do that. You are not ruining their night, I promise!

Imagine your party guests waking up the next morning, reflecting on the event. What do you hope they feel?

Seen, loved, fed! And I would love for them to be inspired to host a gathering in their own home. That’s always the best compliment.
