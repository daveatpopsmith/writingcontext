# Padma Lakshmi, Longer Tables (José Andrés)

Source: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BGNWVEEtg3w
Date: published 2023-09-05; captured 2026-09-01
Channel: Chef José Andrés (~46 min). Captions. Not contacted.

How they talk: Padma answers in story, then a short lander. Sentences run long when she is in a kitchen memory (grandmother's pantry, pickle jars climbing the shelf, two-burner stove, no fridge until 1977). Then she clips it: a two- or three-word close. Contractions throughout. Warmth lives in the domestic fact, not in praising herself. She will say she never went to culinary school and never worked a line. Confidence without a gate. She includes herself in the "we" when she talks about Westerners not listening.

Rhythm: Host over-praises; she does not match the volume. She lets a long scene play, then lands. Pattern is specific object, then what it taught, then one clean beat. Occasional unfussy swear as texture, then she explains. Ends intimate, not branded.

One steal: Put the woman in a real kitchen (who cooked, on what stove, what was in the fridge) before the thesis. Close with a short lander instead of a summary.

Never steal: José's flattery. Super taster as a product claim. Hulu CTA. Weeping-as-content. Chef-as-hero table talk. Do not contact her.

Who is in the room: A chef host talking to a famous friend. Not a 45-year-old who already owns Le Creuset. Steal Padma's cadence, not the room.
