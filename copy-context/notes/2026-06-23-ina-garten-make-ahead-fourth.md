# Ina Garten, Barefoot Contessa Substack — Make-ahead Fourth of July menu

Source: https://inagarten.substack.com/p/make-ahead-fourth-of-july-menu
Date: published 2026-06-23; captured 2026-09-01

How they talk: Warm hostess, not a chef. She names the party, then a timeline so July 4th is a buffet and a beer. Contractions. Store-bought lemonade is allowed out loud. Guests help cook the burgers. The podcast news sits above Ask Ina and does not become the voice.

Rhythm: Welcome. One piece of news. Then the friend's question. Then the menu. Then the three-day plan. Favorites like a person who just went to dinner at someone else's house.

One steal: Give the host a calendar, not a flex. Leave one job for the guests so they feel in it.

Never steal: Cookbook titles as a stack to sell. Podcast announcement as copy. Do not contact her.

Who is in the room: Women throwing a familiar summer party who already own the cookbooks.

Short attributed phrases:
- Ina: "focus on your company"
- Ina: "store-bought is fine!"
- Ina: "no one has to panic about entertaining!"
