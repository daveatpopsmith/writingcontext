Mirror: Google Drive → Popsmith → Copy context
On-disk: `/home/box/shared/copy-context/`

## Two libraries

**Talk like her** (`transcripts/`): long-form host, cook, table voice. How a 45-year-old woman talks. Intimate, I, paragraphs. Ina, Smitten, Cup of Jo, Padma. Label: long-form. Not every script uses this.

**Brand talks to her** (`brand-talk/`): how a brand sells to that woman. Landing pages and product pages in her world: high-end cookware, yoga, women's health supplements, nice furniture. Selling language. Not a confession. Not how to talk like her.

## Capture once

Whole paragraphs and better-context sections. Not notes. Not a full-post dump. Not "one steal" or short attributed phrases.

INDEX catalogs what is saved. Recapture an INDEX URL only to replace a thin or phrase-mined file.

Copy that ships paraphrases. Do not paste Ina or a HexClad lander into a Popsmith piece.
